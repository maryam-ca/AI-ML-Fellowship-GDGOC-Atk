1. Plant Watering Reminder System

Smart Plant Caretaker

Predicts when a house plant needs water using past watering dates and room conditions. It helps avoid overwatering and underwatering.

Dataset: House Plants Care Dataset (UCI)

Features: Plant type, pot size, room temperature, last watered date

Method: Days since last watering + simple moving average

Output: Next watering day suggestion

Why good: Daily-life problem, very simple logic, time-series use
